Our Story

About Magnolia

A place born from a passion for great food, warm hospitality, and unforgettable moments.

How It Began

Born From a Love of Good Moments

Magnolia Coffee & Restaurant was created with a single vision: to give the people of Oran a place that felt truly special. A place where the food is exceptional, the atmosphere is warm, and every visit leaves you with a memory worth keeping.

Located in the heart of Millennium 1, Magnolia quickly became one of Oran's most talked-about dining destinations — earning over 219 Google reviews and a loyal community of 32,000 followers on Instagram who return again and again.

From birthday celebrations to quiet romantic dinners, from family gatherings to business lunches — Magnolia has become the restaurant Oranais choose when the occasion truly matters.

Our Mission

Why We Do What We Do

To create memorable dining experiences that bring people together — through exceptional food, genuine hospitality, and a space that feels like home.

Every dish we serve, every table we set, every event we host — it all comes back to this mission. We believe that a great meal is more than food on a plate. It is a feeling. A conversation. A memory. And we work every day to make sure Magnolia delivers exactly that.
